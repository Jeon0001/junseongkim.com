About

I am a MSc student in the School of Computing at the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST). My interests lie in the intersection of data science, ethical technology and business ethics.


Before joining KAIST, I was based in the United Kingdom where I completed my K-12 education at Magdalen College School, Oxford where I developed a passion for all things STEM.


In my free time, I enjoy playing the guitar, competitive swimming, and cooking. I am currently working on projects based on benchmarking techniques for analyzing cultural awareness of open-source and proprietary LLMs.

My Philosophy

I believe that technology should be used to improve the lives of all people and that it should be developed in an ethical and sustainable manner. I am passionate about using data science to solve the world's most pressing problems and I am always looking for opportunities to use my skills to make a positive impact on the world.


I am also passionate about business ethics and I believe that businesses should be held to the highest ethical standards. I am always open to new opportunities and I am happy to help out with any projects that align with my interests.
