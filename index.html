About

Hello! I am a Master's student at KAIST School of Computing, currently working at U&I Lab advised by Professor Alice Oh.


My current research interests focus on AI alignment, model bias, and evaluation of AI systems. I'm particularly interested in how to make AI systems more transparent, and designing datasets and benchmarks that are able to expose hidden model failures.


Other than research, I'm currently a founder of a startup called Particall, where I'm working on developing a social platform to make it easier for people to find others to play games with. If you're interested, here's a shameless plug @ particall.net!


In my free time, I enjoy the usual, playing guitar, reading, and judging coffee at Cafes. If I interest you at all, shoot me an email @ 09jkim@kaist.ac.kr!

My Philosophy

I believe that technology should be used to improve the lives of all people and that it should be developed in an ethical and sustainable manner. I am passionate about using data science to solve the world's most pressing problems and I am always looking for opportunities to use my skills to make a positive impact on the world.


I am also passionate about business ethics and I believe that businesses should be held to the highest ethical standards. I am always open to new opportunities and I am happy to help out with any projects that align with my interests.
